### 5. Tuljapur & Lohara Thematic GIS Atlas
* **Location:** `05_Tuljapur_Lohara_GIS_Atlas`
* **Focus:** Comprehensive automated pipeline for DEM, contours, slope, stream order, drainage density, LULC, soil depth, roads, climate metrics, and NDVI for Tuljapur and Lohara blocks.
* **Scripts & Pipeline:** Complete Python toolchain (`src/` with `01_data_download.py` through `run_pipeline.py`).
* **Deliverables:** Cartographic maps, invoice, and thematic spatial layers.

### 6. Phaltan Village GIS Mapping & Watershed Proposal
* **Location:** `06_Phaltan_Village_GIS_Mapping`
* **Focus:** Watershed delineation, hydrological DEM modeling, contour processing, and cartographic map composition for 6 Phaltan villages (Bodkewadi, Daryachiwadi, Pirachiwadi, Tardaf, Tathvade, Waghoshi).
* **Delivered Files:**
  * `Outputs/SJSM_phaltan_Proposal_Maps.zip` (213.5 MB master map bundle)
  * `Outputs/village_statistics.csv` (Tabulated hydrological and terrain statistics)
  * `Outputs/SJSM_phaltan_Proposal_Maps/` (Individual high-resolution proposal map sheets)
* **Interactive Projects:** QGIS Project (`SJSM_MAPPING.qgz`) and ArcGIS Document (`Mappping Layouts.mxd`).
* **Scripts:** `map_renderer.py` and `run_ultimate_pipeline.py`.

### 7. Biodiversity Park Layout Project
* **Location:** `07_Biodiversity_Park_Layout_Project`
* **Focus:** Master spatial planning and zoning for the SJSM Biodiversity Park, integrating soil & water conservation structures with native agroforestry.
* **Delivered Files:** 7 Production KML layers in `kml_layouts/` (compatible with Google Earth, QGIS, and ArcGIS):
  * `Building.kml` (Administrative & visitor facilities)
  * `CNB.kml` (Cement Nala Bund / check dam structure)
  * `Farm Pond total 6.kml` (6 Planned rainwater harvesting farm ponds)
  * `Jalkund.kml` (Micro-water storage structure)
  * `Park Roads.kml` (Internal circulation paths and walkways)
  * `Plantation on CCT.kml` (Continuous Contour Trenches afforestation zones)
  * `Plantation.kml` (Primary native flora tree canopy zones)
