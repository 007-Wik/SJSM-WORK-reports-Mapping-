**Prepared for:** Shramjivi Janata Sahayak Mandal (SJSM)

## 🌿 Executive Summary

We are pleased to present the master spatial zoning and infrastructure layout for the **SJSM Biodiversity Park Project**. This initiative integrates ecological restoration, rainwater harvesting, soil conservation, and community eco-tourism into an engineered spatial layout.

All spatial features have been precisely mapped using GPS field measurements and satellite elevation modeling. The layers are ready for seamless deployment into **Google Earth Pro**, **QGIS**, **ArcGIS**, or handheld GPS navigators for ground execution and donor presentations.

## 🎯 Primary Final Deliverables

The complete set of GIS spatial layout layers is located in the [`kml_layouts/`](./kml_layouts/) directory:

| Deliverable Layer | File Name | Description & Field Specification |
|---|---|---|
| **Pond Infrastructure** | [`Farm Pond total 6.kml`](./kml_layouts/Farm%20Pond%20total%206.kml) | Boundary delineations and coordinates for all **6 planned Farm Ponds** strategically positioned at catchment depressions to maximize surface runoff collection. |
| **Micro-Water Harvesting** | [`Jalkund.kml`](./kml_layouts/Jalkund.kml) | Targeted coordinates for high-elevation earthen water-storage structures (Jalkund) dedicated to supporting young saplings during dry spells. |
| **Check Dam / Bunding** | [`CNB.kml`](./kml_layouts/CNB.kml) | Exact site placement for the Cement Nala Bund (CNB) designed for stream gully stabilization and groundwater recharge. |
| **Contour Agroforestry** | [`Plantation on CCT.kml`](./kml_layouts/Plantation%20on%20CCT.kml) | Afforestation and agroforestry layout aligned along Continuous Contour Trenches (CCT) for moisture retention and slope stabilization. |
| **Main Flora Canopy** | [`Plantation.kml`](./kml_layouts/Plantation.kml) | Boundary zoning and planting density framework for native tree species, medicinal plants, and fruit canopies. |
| **Internal Access Roads** | [`Park Roads.kml`](./kml_layouts/Park%20Roads.kml) | Planned internal access paths, maintenance tracks, and visitor walking trails designed to minimize soil compaction. |
| **Administrative & Visitor Facilities** | [`Building.kml`](./kml_layouts/Building.kml) | Geo-tagged footprint for the park office, interpretation center, nursery shed, and sanitation facilities. |

## 🗺️ How to View & Use These Deliverables

### Option A: Google Earth Pro (Recommended for Quick Visuals & Presentations)
1. Launch **Google Earth Pro** (Desktop or Web).
2. Go to **File > Open** (or drag and drop all `.kml` files from `kml_layouts/`).
3. Expand the layers under **Places** in the left sidebar to toggle individual layers (e.g., viewing Ponds with CCT plantations).

### Option B: QGIS / ArcGIS (For Cartography & Spatial Analysis)
1. Open your GIS project.
2. Drag and drop any `.kml` layer directly into the Map Canvas.
3. Reproject to **WGS 84 / UTM Zone 43N (EPSG: 32643)** if performing area, perimeter, or volume calculations.
